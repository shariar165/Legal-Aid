EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT FOR FOREIGN WORKER

1. Position and Term. The Worker is employed as a Machine Operator at the Employer's factory in Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia for a period of three (3) years.

2. Salary. The Employer shall pay the Worker a basic salary of RM 1,200 per month, payable on the 10th day of the following month.

3. Deductions. (a) recovery of recruitment, visa processing and agency costs of RM 3,000, at RM 250 per month for 12 months; (b) accommodation charge of RM 200 per month; (c) transport to the factory, RM 100 per month; (d) medical and insurance contribution of RM 100 per month.

4. Passport. Upon arrival in Malaysia the Worker shall surrender his passport to the Employer, and the passport shall be kept in the Employer's custody for safekeeping throughout the term of this Contract.

5. Working Hours. Normal working hours are twelve (12) hours per day, six (6) days per week. Overtime is payable at the Employer's discretion.

6. Travel. The Worker shall bear the cost of his own air ticket to Malaysia.

7. Termination. The Employer may terminate this Contract at any time without notice if the Worker is found unsuitable.

8. Complaints and Disputes. The Worker shall not file any complaint or case with BMET, the Bangladesh High Commission or any court in Bangladesh in respect of this Contract.

9. Governing Law. This Contract is governed by the laws of Malaysia.
