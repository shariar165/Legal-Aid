Based on an audit of `main` at 15c631a. A work order like spec 28: each numbered step is one branch and one PR.
There is no calendar. Do the phases in order, and if time runs short, stop at the "Must-do line" at the end.

- AGENTS.md hard rules hold: no hand-typed statute text or section numbers (corpus scripts only), citations only
  from retrieval, the 4 gates and the drafter's stripping untouched, the PII shield on every outbound path, free
  OpenRouter models only, one identical bench verdict prompt, NFC Bangla, no real company in a demo document.

  `python scripts/check/contrast.py`, and the step 0.1 audit on the states the step touches, at 390, 820 and
  1440 px, light and dark, with before/after screenshots in `docs/design/`.

  limitations" true.
- Live calls (warm-ups, smoke runs) at night only. Never spend paid credit. Deploy order: Railway, check

- **7.7 Notice mode** `notice-mode` [PROMPT][API][UI] (the brief's 3rd example: a photo of a notice → a filing
  timeline)

    destination.
