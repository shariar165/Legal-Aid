MANPOWER SUPPLY AND EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Meghna Bridge Overseas Ltd. (fictional), RL No. 0000 (fictional), Purana Paltan, Dhaka, Bangladesh (the "Agency"), and Najd Crest Facilities Services Co. (fictional), Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (the "Company"). It sets the terms on which the Agency recruits Bangladeshi workers for the Company and the terms of each Worker's employment with the Company in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

PART 1 - DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "Agreement" means this Manpower Supply and Employment Agreement, its Parts, Schedules and any written amendment signed by both Parties.
1.2 "Agency" means the recruiting agency named in the preamble, licensed in Bangladesh to recruit workers for overseas employment.
1.3 "Company" means the employer named in the preamble, a facilities and construction services company in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
1.4 "Worker" means each person recruited by the Agency and employed by the Company under this Agreement, as listed in the Mobilisation Schedule.
1.5 "Mobilisation Schedule" means the list of selected Workers, their trades and arrival dates issued under clause 2.5.
1.6 "Site" means any project, facility or premises listed in Schedule 2 or later notified by the Company.
1.7 "Camp" means the accommodation provided or arranged by the Company for Workers.
1.8 "Basic Salary" means the fixed monthly amount stated for the Worker's trade in Schedule 1, excluding allowances and overtime.
1.9 "Allowances" means any food, transport or other allowance stated in Schedule 1 or in a written notice of the Company.
1.10 "Overtime" means hours worked beyond the normal hours stated in clause 5.1.
1.11 "Iqama" means the residence permit issued to a non-Saudi worker in the Kingdom.
1.12 "Work Permit" means the permit allowing the Worker to work for the Company in the Kingdom.
1.13 "Probation Period" means the first ninety (90) days after the Worker's arrival.
1.14 "Contract Term" means two (2) years from the Worker's date of arrival in the Kingdom, unless ended earlier under Part 9.
1.15 "Recruitment Costs" means all fees, charges and expenses of selection, documentation, visa, medical examination, training and travel to the Kingdom.
1.16 "Supervisor" means the person appointed by the Company to direct the Worker's daily work at a Site.
1.17 "Disciplinary Code" means the code of conduct and penalties set out in Schedule 4.
1.18 "Grievance" means any complaint by a Worker about pay, conditions, treatment or safety.
1.19 "Force Majeure Event" means an event beyond the reasonable control of a Party, including war, epidemic, flood and government action.
1.20 "Business Day" means a day other than a Friday, Saturday or official holiday in the Kingdom.
1.21 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information about a Party, its clients, prices, methods and personnel.
1.22 "Replacement Worker" means a worker supplied by the Agency in place of a Worker who leaves during the Probation Period.
1.23 "Service Fee" means the fee payable by the Company to the Agency for each Worker supplied, as stated in clause 2.9.
1.24 "PPE" means the personal protective equipment listed in Schedule 3.
1.25 "Training Plan" means the courses listed in Schedule 7.
1.26 "Accommodation Standards" means the minimum standards for the Camp set out in Schedule 5.
1.27 "Kingdom" means the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
1.28 "Parties" means the Agency and the Company, and where the context requires, the Worker.
1.29 Headings are for convenience only. Words in the singular include the plural, and a reference to a clause or Schedule is to a clause of or Schedule to this Agreement.

PART 2 - SUPPLY OF WORKERS

2.1 The Company appoints the Agency to recruit Workers in Bangladesh for employment at the Sites, and the Agency accepts the appointment.
2.2 The Company shall issue a demand letter stating the number of Workers, their trades, the Basic Salary for each trade and the Contract Term.
2.3 The Agency shall obtain every permission required by the law of Bangladesh before advertising or selecting any Worker.
2.4 The Agency shall select Workers through trade tests and interviews, and the Company may send a representative to attend them.
2.5 The Agency shall issue a Mobilisation Schedule for each batch of Workers following the timeline in Schedule 6.
2.6 The Agency shall ensure that each Worker has a valid passport, a medical fitness certificate and emigration clearance before departure.
2.7 The Agency shall give each Worker a pre-departure orientation on the Kingdom, the Site rules and this Agreement.
2.8 If a Worker leaves during the Probation Period, the Agency shall supply a Replacement Worker within sixty (60) days.
2.9 The Company shall pay the Agency a Service Fee of SAR 1,500 per Worker within thirty (30) days of the Worker's arrival.
2.10 The Agency shall not take any money from a Worker except as allowed by the law of Bangladesh.
2.11 The Agency shall keep a copy of each Worker's employment terms and send a copy to the authorities as the law requires.
2.12 Each Party shall comply with the laws of Bangladesh and of the Kingdom applicable to its obligations under this Agreement.

PART 3 - THE WORKER'S DOCUMENTS AND OBLIGATIONS

3.1 The Worker shall work diligently in his trade at any Site to which he is assigned and follow the reasonable instructions of his Supervisor.
3.2 The Worker shall respect the laws, customs and religion of the Kingdom.
3.3 The Worker shall keep his identity card and Site pass with him while at a Site.
3.4 Passport. On arrival in the Kingdom the Worker shall deposit his passport with the Company, which shall keep it in safe custody for the whole Contract Term and release it only on final exit.
3.5 The Worker shall not work for any other person during the Contract Term.
3.6 The Worker shall attend the medical examinations the Company requires.
3.7 The Worker shall take care of Company property, tools and vehicles entrusted to him.
3.8 The Worker shall inform the Company within seven (7) days of any change in his family contact details.

PART 4 - REMUNERATION AND DEDUCTIONS

4.1 The Company shall pay the Worker the Basic Salary for his trade stated in Schedule 1. For a General Labourer the Basic Salary is SAR 1,000 (Saudi Riyals One Thousand) per month.
4.2 Salary is paid monthly in arrears by bank transfer or payroll card not later than the seventh (7th) day of the following month.
4.3 The Company shall give each Worker a monthly pay slip showing the Basic Salary, Allowances, Overtime and every deduction.
4.4 Deductions. The Worker agrees that the following amounts shall be deducted from his monthly salary:
(a) recovery of recruitment, visa and agency costs of SAR 4,800, at SAR 400 per month for 12 months;
(b) accommodation charge of SAR 150 per month;
(c) food charge of SAR 100 per month.
4.5 Overtime approved in writing is paid at the rate in Schedule 1.
4.6 The Company may change the date of payment by giving one month's written notice.
4.7 Any amount the Worker owes the Company may be set off against his salary.
4.8 Residence Permit. The Worker shall pay the cost of renewing his iqama (residence permit) and work permit every year.

PART 5 - WORKING TIME AND LEAVE

5.1 Normal working hours are eight (8) hours per day and forty-eight (48) hours per week, reduced to six (6) hours per day during Ramadan for Muslim Workers.
5.2 Friday is the weekly rest day unless the Supervisor assigns another day.
5.3 The Worker is entitled to twenty-one (21) days of paid annual leave after one year of service.
5.4 The Worker is entitled to paid sick leave on a medical certificate in accordance with the law of the Kingdom.
5.5 The Worker may take leave only on dates approved by the Company.

PART 6 - ACCOMMODATION, TRANSPORT AND MEDICAL CARE

6.1 The Company shall provide accommodation meeting the Accommodation Standards in Schedule 5.
6.2 The Company shall provide daily transport between the Camp and the Site.
6.3 The Company shall provide medical insurance for each Worker as required by the law of the Kingdom.
6.4 The Company shall provide the air ticket from Dhaka to Riyadh.

PART 7 - HEALTH AND SAFETY

7.1 The Company shall provide each Worker with the PPE listed in Schedule 3 free of charge and replace worn or damaged items promptly.
7.2 The Worker shall wear the PPE required for each task and shall not remove or tamper with any guard or safety device.
7.3 No work in direct sunlight shall be carried out between 12:00 and 15:00 from 15 June to 15 September, in line with the midday work ban of the Kingdom.
7.4 The Company shall provide cool drinking water, shaded rest areas and electrolyte drinks at every Site.
7.5 A Worker who feels unwell from heat shall stop work and report to the Supervisor, and shall not be penalised for doing so.
7.6 Work at height above two (2) metres requires a permit, a trained Worker and a full-body harness attached to a certified anchor point.
7.7 Scaffolds shall be erected, altered and dismantled only by trained scaffolders and inspected before each shift.
7.8 Hot work requires a permit, a fire watch and a fire extinguisher within reach for the whole duration of the work.
7.9 Entry into a confined space requires a permit, gas testing and a standby attendant outside the space.
7.10 Electrical work shall be done only by qualified electricians under a lock-out and tag-out procedure.
7.11 Every incident, near miss and injury shall be reported to the Supervisor on the same shift and recorded by the Company.
7.12 The Company shall provide first aid at every Site and transport an injured Worker to a clinic or hospital without delay and at its own cost.
7.13 Workers shall attend the courses of the Training Plan in Schedule 7 during working hours.
7.14 Vehicles used to transport Workers shall have a seat for each passenger, air conditioning and a valid inspection certificate.
7.15 Smoking is not allowed at any Site except in marked areas away from flammable materials.
7.16 Chemicals shall be stored in labelled containers and used only with the safety data sheet available at the place of use.
7.17 Excavations deeper than one point two (1.2) metres shall be shored, sloped or benched before any Worker enters.
7.18 Lifting operations shall follow a lift plan, and no Worker shall stand under a suspended load.
7.19 The Company shall carry out a safety inspection of each Site at least weekly and keep the report for two (2) years.
7.20 A Worker may refuse work that he reasonably believes presents a serious and imminent danger to his life or health.

PART 8 - CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINE

8.1 The Disciplinary Code in Schedule 4 forms part of this Agreement and binds every Worker.
8.2 The Supervisor may issue verbal and written warnings. Deductions and dismissals are decided by the Company's site manager.
8.3 A Worker may appeal a penalty in writing to the Company's human resources manager within seven (7) days.

PART 9 - TERMINATION

9.1 Either Party may end a Worker's employment during the Probation Period without notice.
9.2 The Company may terminate a Worker's employment at any time without notice if the Worker is found unsuitable.
9.3 The Worker may resign by giving the Company two (2) months' written notice.
9.4 The employment ends automatically on the expiry of the Contract Term unless renewed in writing.
9.5 On termination the Company shall pay all wages due up to the last day worked, less any amounts owed by the Worker.
9.6 Travel. The Worker shall bear the cost of his own return ticket to Bangladesh at the end of this Contract.

PART 10 - END OF SERVICE

10.1 The Worker shall not be entitled to any end-of-service award or gratuity on the completion or termination of this Contract.
10.2 The Company shall issue a service certificate on request.

PART 11 - COMPLAINTS, DISPUTES AND GOVERNING LAW

11.1 Complaints and Disputes. The Worker waives all claims against the Company and the Agency that are not raised in writing within seven (7) days, and shall not file any complaint or case with BMET, the Bangladesh Embassy or any court in Bangladesh in respect of this Contract.
11.2 Disputes between the Agency and the Company shall be settled amicably, failing which by the competent courts of Riyadh.
11.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
11.4 The Parties shall cooperate with any inquiry by the authorities of Bangladesh or the Kingdom about the Workers.

PART 12 - GENERAL

12.1 Notices. Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing in English and delivered by hand, courier or email to the address of the receiving Party stated in the preamble, and is deemed received on the next Business Day after sending.
12.2 Assignment. Neither the Agency nor the Company may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
12.3 Subcontracting. The Company may assign Workers to work at Sites of its clients, provided the Company remains the employer and remains responsible for all payments due to the Workers.
12.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, its Parts and Schedules constitute the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject and supersede all prior negotiations, representations and understandings.
12.5 Amendment. No amendment of this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by authorised representatives of the Agency and the Company.
12.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force, and the invalid provision shall be replaced by a valid provision closest to its intent.
12.7 Waiver. A failure or delay by a Party to exercise any right under this Agreement is not a waiver of that right, and a single or partial exercise does not prevent any further exercise.
12.8 Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.
12.9 Confidentiality. Each Party shall keep the Confidential Information of the other Party confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement, during the Contract Term and for three (3) years afterwards.
12.10 Anti-Bribery. Neither Party shall offer, give, request or accept any bribe, facilitation payment or improper advantage in connection with this Agreement.
12.11 Insurance. The Company shall maintain public liability insurance for its Sites and motor insurance for vehicles used to transport Workers.
12.12 Audit. The Company may, on reasonable notice, inspect the Agency's records relating to the selection and documentation of the Workers supplied under this Agreement.
12.13 Records. The Company shall keep payroll and attendance records for each Worker for the Contract Term and two (2) years afterwards.
12.14 Language. This Agreement is made in English. Any translation is for convenience only and the English text prevails.
12.15 Costs. Each Party bears its own costs of negotiating and signing this Agreement.
12.16 Relationship. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership or agency relationship between the Agency and the Company, except as required by the law applicable to the recruitment of the Workers.
12.17 Further Assurance. Each Party shall sign such further documents and do such further acts as may reasonably be required to give effect to this Agreement.
12.18 Data. The Company may keep copies of the Workers' identity documents, photographs and medical reports for employment purposes and to comply with the law of the Kingdom.
12.19 Announcements. Neither Party shall make any public announcement about this Agreement without the written approval of the other Party.
12.20 Survival. Clauses 9.6, 10.1, 11.1 to 11.4 and 12.9 survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement.

SCHEDULE 4 - DISCIPLINARY CODE

4.1 Late arrival at the Site without a reasonable excuse: written warning; on the third occasion, deduction of one hour's pay.
4.2 Absence without notice for one day: deduction of the day's pay and a written warning.
4.3 Sleeping on duty: written warning; on repetition, deduction of one day's pay.
4.4 Smoking outside marked areas: written warning.
4.5 Failure to wear required PPE: written warning; on repetition, suspension for one day without pay.
4.6 Fighting or threatening behaviour at a Site or in the Camp: final written warning or dismissal after investigation.
4.7 Absconding. A Worker who leaves the worksite or the Camp without the Company's written permission for more than three (3) days shall be deemed to have absconded. The absconding Worker shall pay the Company liquidated damages of SAR 10,000 together with all outstanding Recruitment Costs, and the Company may report him to the authorities as absconded and withhold any wages due to him until the full sum is paid.
4.8 Damage to Company property through negligence: the Worker pays the cost of repair, recovered from his salary.
4.9 Theft or fraud: dismissal without notice or end-of-service benefits, and a report to the police.
4.10 Possession or use of alcohol or drugs: dismissal without notice and a report to the authorities.
4.11 Disclosure of Confidential Information: final written warning or dismissal.
4.12 Every penalty shall be recorded in writing, and the Worker shall be given an opportunity to explain before it is imposed.
