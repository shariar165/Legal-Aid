AL-WADI BUILDING CONTRACTING CO. (fictional) — RESPONSIBLE RECRUITMENT AND WORKER WELFARE POLICY

Issued by the Human Resources Department, Riyadh. This is a fictional sample policy for demonstration only.

1. Scope. This policy applies to every worker we employ in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, whether recruited directly or through a recruitment agency in the worker's home country.

2. No recruitment fees. We follow the Employer Pays Principle. No worker shall pay any recruitment fee or related cost to get or keep a job with us. The company bears all recruitment, visa, iqama, work permit and agency costs, and will reimburse any worker who was charged such fees.

3. Identity documents. The company will never hold or keep a worker's passport or identity documents. Passports remain with the worker at all times.

4. Travel. The company pays the air ticket to Saudi Arabia and the return ticket to the worker's home country at the end of the contract.

5. Wages. Wages are paid in full every month into the worker's own bank account. No deduction is made except those required by law or agreed by the worker in writing.

6. End of service. Every worker receives the end-of-service award required by the Saudi Labor Law when the contract ends.

7. Grievances. Every worker has the right to raise a grievance with the company, the Labour Office or the embassy of their country without fear of retaliation.

Approved by the Board of Directors.
