SUPPLY AGREEMENT (CONTROL DOCUMENT - COMPLIANT TERMS)

Between Nordwind Fashion GmbH, Hamburg ("Buyer") and Delta Knit Ltd. (fictional), Gazipur, Bangladesh ("Supplier"), a factory located outside any export processing zone.

1. Payment Terms. Payment shall be made by irrevocable letter of credit, at sight, and in any event within ninety (90) days of the date of shipment.

2. Delay. In case of delay the Buyer may claim reasonable compensation for loss actually suffered and proved.

3. Code of Conduct. The Buyer shall share the reasonable costs of independent audits and shall provide capacity-building support to the Supplier.

4. Governing Law and Jurisdiction. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Bangladesh and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in Dhaka.

5. Termination of Workers. Any termination of a permanent worker shall be made with one hundred and twenty (120) days' written notice as required by law.

6. Maternity. Female workers receive maternity benefit for sixty (60) days before and sixty (60) days after delivery.
