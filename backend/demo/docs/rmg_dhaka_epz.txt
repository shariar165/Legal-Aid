PART A - SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement is made between Nordwind Fashion GmbH, Hamburg, Germany ("Buyer") and Lumen Apparel (BD) Ltd. (fictional), Plot 21, Dhaka Export Processing Zone (DEPZ), Ganakbari, Savar, Dhaka, Bangladesh ("Supplier"), a Type B industrial unit operating inside the Dhaka EPZ.

1. Products. The Supplier shall manufacture and export knitted garments to the Buyer in accordance with purchase orders issued from time to time.

2. Payment Terms. The Buyer shall pay each invoice one hundred and fifty (150) days after the date of shipment (bill of lading date), by telegraphic transfer.

3. Delay. If any shipment is delayed, the Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of 20% of the order value for each week of delay, without the Buyer being required to prove any loss.

4. Code of Conduct and Due Diligence. The Supplier shall comply with the Buyer's Supplier Code of Conduct and the Buyer's human rights due diligence programme under the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive. The Supplier shall bear all costs of audits, due diligence, corrective action plans and remediation required by the Buyer, at its own cost.

5. Freedom of Association. The Supplier shall recognise any trade union freely chosen by its workers, shall allow multiple unions at the factory, and shall bargain collectively with them.

6. Worker Data. The Supplier shall share personal data of its workers, including names, ages, wages, health records and grievance records, with the Buyer and the Buyer's auditors in the European Union upon request.

7. Exclusivity and Non-Compete. During this Agreement and for two (2) years after its termination, the Supplier shall not manufacture garments for any competitor of the Buyer in the European Union.

8. Claims. Any claim by the Supplier against the Buyer must be brought within three (3) months of the relevant shipment, after which it is time-barred.

9. Termination. The Buyer may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect if the Supplier breaches the Code of Conduct.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
